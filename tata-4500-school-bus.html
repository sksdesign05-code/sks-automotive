Safety & Child-Centric Engineering Architecture

Every millimeter of the TATA 4500 WB School Coach is fabricated with zero tolerance for mechanical compromises, strictly conforming to AIS-063 Rev. 2 and CMVR guidelines.

architecture 01 / STRUCTURAL

Roll-Over Tubular Monocoque Cage

Engineered with cold-drawn high-tensile ERW structural steel square pipes (AIS-052 & AIS-063 compliant). Certified structural survival space retention prevents intrusion into student seating compartments in severe rollover conditions.

chair 02 / INTERIOR

Child-Safe Ergonomic High-Density Seating

Fire-retardant heavy-duty vinyl upholstery, high-density PU molded cushions, impact-absorbing PU foam padding on all exposed grab handles, and integrated under-seat satchel storage baskets.

traffic 03 / EXTERIOR

High-Visibility School Livery & Dual Stop Arm

Finished in high-luminosity Highway School Bus Yellow polyurethane enamel with 3M Diamond Grade micro-prismatic retro-reflective tape. Equipped with pneumatic driver-actuated LED flashing STOP sign arm.

sensor_door 04 / ACCESS

Low Anti-Pinch Pneumatic Entrance Door

Engineered with a low 860 mm entrance step with ribbed anti-skid floor runners and assist grab-rails calibrated for kindergarten & junior school transit. Sensitive optical anti-pinch edge reverses door instantly upon obstacle contact.

videocam 05 / TELEMATICS

AI CCTV & Telematics Surveillance

Factory integrated with 4-channel interior dome infrared cameras, reverse monitoring camera, 30-day solid-state NVR, AIS-140 certified GPS telematics unit, and emergency panic buttons at every seat row.

local_fire_department 06 / FIRE SAFETY

Speed Governor & Fire Detection System

Equipped with an electronic CMVR speed limiter pegged strictly to 40 km/h, automatic thermal engine bay Fire Detection & Suppression System (FDSS), dual ABC dry powder extinguishers, and emergency glass breakers.
