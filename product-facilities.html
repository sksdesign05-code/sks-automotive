Interactive Machinery & Process Grid: 6 Core Stations

Every vehicle superstructure engineered by SKS Automotive travels across calibrated physical manufacturing stages designed for structural equilibrium, metallurgical integrity, and precision tolerance.

STATION 01
Metallurgical Forming

CNC 3-Axis Pipe Bending

Precision cold-bending of primary roll-cage and roof hoop tubes. Guarantees repeatable compound radii with zero cross-sectional ovality or micro-cracking across high-stress junctions.

Tolerance: ±0.15mm TATA Steel ERW
STATION 02
Structural Assembly

Precision MIG / TIG Welding & Jigs

Rigid full-length chassis fixtures anchor the tubular steel skeleton during weld execution, eliminating thermal warping and satisfying ARAI roll-over safety specifications.

Argon 80/20 Shielding AIS-052 Compliant
STATION 03
Stamping Division

Hydraulic Pressing & Deep Draw

High-tonnage hydraulic presses stamping reinforced structural pillars, door gussets, and suspension mounting brackets with standardized dimensional uniformity.

Press Force: 500 Tons High-Yield Steel
STATION 04
Surface Finishing

Pre-Treatment & 80°C Baking Oven

Multistage chemical phosphate dip followed by anti-corrosive epoxy primer and polyurethane coating baked in a sealed 80°C booth for 10-year gloss and corrosion defense.

Bake Temp: 80°C Continuous 1,000hr Salt Spray
STATION 05
Environmental Integrity

Monsoonal Shower Testing Booth

Multi-nozzle 360-degree deluge test recreating intense subcontinental tropical rainfall at 2.5 bar pressure to guarantee 100% leak-proof cabin and window seal containment.

Spray Nozzles: 64 Array Zero-Ingress Pass
STATION 06
Telematics & Integration

Electrical & Systems Harnessing

Automotive fire-resistant cross-linked wiring loops, centralized relay fuse modules, CAN-bus integration, and full preparation for AIS-140 emergency GPS telemetry.

Standard: AIS-140 Tinned Copper XLPE
